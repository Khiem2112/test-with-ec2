## Outcome & Scope

- Actor: Voter (Khach hang, Nguoi dung cuoi), Internal Team Member (Product Manager, Developer, Moderator, Admin).
- Trigger: Nguoi dung muon ung ho mot tinh nang mong muon, nguoi dung tham gia thao luan cong khai duoi bai viet, hoac doi ngu ky thuat can trao doi noi bo ve giai phap va do phuc tap ngay tren bai dang.
- Job to be done: Cung cap co che bieu quyet cong bang co tinh den gia tri khach hang (trong so theo goi dich vu), dong thoi xay dung khong gian trao doi da chieu giup lam ro yeu cau tinh nang ma van bao mat thong tin ky thuat noi bo.
- Successful outcome: Luot bo phieu duoc cap nhat ngay lap tuc tren giao dien voi tong diem trong so chinh xac; binh luan cong khai duoc xep tang theo cay phan cap; ghi chu ky thuat noi bo duoc hien thi rieng biet va an toan cho nhan su noi bo.
- In scope:
  - Thao tac Bo phieu / Huy bo phieu (Vote / Unvote Toggle) voi co che cap nhat giao dien lac quan (Optimistic UI).
  - Tinh toan tong so luot binh chon (`totalVotesCount`) va tong diem trong so (`totalScoreWeight`) theo cap bac tai khoan (Standard: 1.00, Pro: 3.00, Enterprise: 10.00).
  - Ghi nhan nhat ky hoat dong binh chon vao `vote_activity_logs` kem dia chi IP hash va User Agent de phat hien gian lan spam bot.
  - Thao luan dang cay da tang (Threaded Nested Comments) ho tro tra loi truc tiep binh luan cha (`parentId`).
  - Phim tat danh gia cam xuc tren binh luan (Comment Reactions: THUMBS_UP, THUMBS_DOWN, HEART, ROCKET, INSIGHTFUL).
  - Che do tao va doc ghi chu ky thuat noi bo (`is_internal_note = true`) danh rieng cho thanh vien to chuc.
  - Tu dong them nguoi tham gia binh luan hoac bo phieu vao danh sach theo doi (`post_subscriptions`).
- Out of scope:
  - Tich hop cong cu sua binh luan thoi gian thuc dang rich-text WYSIWYG phuc tap (chi su dung dinh dang Markdown tieu chuan).
  - Chuc nang tag ten (@mention) tu dong goi y thanh vien he thong (se bo sung o phien ban sau).

## Context & Constraints

- Preconditions: Nguoi dung da duoc xac thuc VoterProfile; bai dang khong bi khoa (`is_locked = false`) va o trang thai hoat dong (`is_active = true`).
- Source of truth: Bang `votes`, `vote_activity_logs`, `comments`, `comment_reactions`, va `voter_profiles`.
- Permissions & ownership:
  - Moi VoterProfile co the thuc hien bo phieu 1 lan tren moi bai dang.
  - Binh luan cong khai mo cho tat ca nguoi dung da xac thuc.
  - Chi tai khoan la thanh vien Workspace thuoc bang `workspace_members` moi co quyen gui va xem cac binh luan co `is_internal_note = true`.
- Consequential actions:
  - Thao tac Vote/Unvote phai chay trong giao dich Database nguyen tu de cap nhat dong thoi bang `votes` va hai truong tong hop tren `posts`.
  - Luot vote dau tien cua VoterProfile tren bai dang se tu dong tao ban ghi theo doi trong `post_subscriptions`.

## State Model

| State | Meaning | Permitted Operator Actions | Terminal / Exit Condition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Primary Path (Happy Path)

### Luong Bo Phieu Thong Minh (Smart Voting Path)

- Step Initiation:
  - User Action: Khach hang bam vao nut Upvote tren the bai dang de xuat tinh nang "Xuat bao cao PDF".
  - System Response: Giao dien ngay lap tuc cap nhat trang thai nut sang mau xanh chu dao va tang diem hien thi them 1 (Cap nhat lac quan - Optimistic UI).

- Step Configuration:
  - User Action: Yeu cau goi API duoc gui len may chu (`POST /api/posts/[id]/vote`).
  - System Response:
    - Backend mo Database Transaction.
    - Kiem tra ban ghi `Vote` voi khoa duy nhat `(postId, voterProfileId)`.
    - Doc trong so cua VoterProfile tu co so du lieu (vi du: Tai khoan Enterprise co `voteWeight = 5.00`).
    - Vi chua ton tai, backend chen ban ghi moi vao bang `votes` voi `appliedWeight = 5.00`.
    - Cap nhat nguyen tu: `totalVotesCount = totalVotesCount + 1` va `totalScoreWeight = totalScoreWeight + 5.00`.
    - Ghi nhat ky vao `vote_activity_logs` voi hanh dong `UPVOTE`, ma bam IP va User Agent.
    - Kiem tra va tu dong tao `post_subscriptions` neu chua co.
    - Commit Transaction thanh cong.

- Step Confirmation & Completion:
  - User Action: Nguoi dung nhin thay diem so chinh xac sau khi may chu phan hoi, kem trang thai "Da binh chon".
  - System Response: Tra ve HTTP 200 OK kem du lieu diem so moi nhat cho client.

### Luong Trao Doi Cong Khai Va Phan Hoi Phan Cap (Nested Comments)

- Step Initiation:
  - User Action: Nguoi dung cuon xuong phan binh luan cua bai viet va nhap y kien dong gop vao khung soan thao.
  - System Response: O soan thao Markdown mo rong, hien thi cac nut dinh dang co ban va ban xem truoc.

- Step Configuration:
  - User Action: Nguoi dung go noi dung va nhan nut Gui Binh Luan. (Hoac nhan nut Tra Loi duoi mot binh luan cu the de tao phan hoi cap con voi `parentId`).
  - System Response: Backend kiem tra tinh hop le cua du lieu (noi dung khong trong, do dai tu 3 den 5000 ky tu), luu ban ghi vao bang `comments` voi `is_internal_note = false`.

- Step Confirmation & Completion:
  - User Action: Binh luan moi xuat hien ngay tuc thi duoi luong thao luan voi hinh anh dai dien va ten tac gia.
  - System Response:
    - Tang chi so `commentsCount` tren bang `posts`.
    - Tu dong dang ky theo doi cho nguoi binh luan vao bang `post_subscriptions`.
    - Day su kien thong bao toi cac thanh vien dang theo doi bai viet.

### Luong Ghi Chu Ky Thuat Noi Bo (Private Team Note)

- Step Initiation:
  - User Action: Product Manager truy cap bai dang cua khach hang tren he thong va chuyen sang tab "Ghi Chu Noi Bo" tren o soan thao.
  - System Response: Khung soan thao doi sang vien canh bao mau vang voi dong chu: "Ghi chu noi bo: Chi nhan su to chuc moi co quyen xem".

- Step Configuration:
  - User Action: PM nhap danh gia: "Kha thi cao, da co module PDF tai backend, can khoang 2 ngay lam viec cua doi Frontend", sau do bam Luu Ghi Chu.
  - System Response:
    - Backend kiem tra phan quyen: Xac nhan tai khoan nguoi dung hien tai thuoc bang `workspace_members`.
    - Luu ban ghi vao bang `comments` voi `is_internal_note = true`.

- Step Confirmation & Completion:
  - User Action: Ghi chu xuat hien tren dong thoi gian cua PM voi bieu tuong o khoa van ban va vien canh bao ro rang.
  - System Response: Khi khach hang ben ngoai truy cap bai viet, truy van SQL tai Backend tu dong ap dung menh de dieu kien `WHERE is_internal_note = false`, hoan toan khong lo thong tin ra ngoai.

## Exception & Recovery Paths

- Concurrency Vote Conflict Path:
  - Trigger: Nguoi dung bam nut Upvote lien tuc nhieu lan trong thoi gian cuc ngan dan den hai yeu cau den may chu dong thoi.
  - System Behavior: Co so du lieu chan xung dot bang khoa duy nhat `@@unique([postId, voterProfileId])`. Giao dich xung dot bi rollback, he thong tra ve trang thai hop nhat cuoi cung ma khong gay sai lech diem so `totalVotesCount`.

- Unauthorized Internal Note Submission Path:
  - Trigger: Nguoi dung thong thuong (khong phai thanh vien noi bo) co tinh gui payload co chua tham so `is_internal_note = true` len API.
  - System Behavior: Backend xac thuc quyen han qua Session va WorkspaceMember. Neu khong phai la thanh vien to chuc, he thong tu dong bo qua co nay hoac tu choi yeu cau voi ma loi HTTP 403 Forbidden.

- Locked Post Comment Attempt Path:
  - Trigger: Nguoi dung gui binh luan vao bai dang vua bi khoa thao luan boi quan tri vien.
  - System Behavior: API kiem tra co `is_locked = true` cua `posts`, tu choi luu binh luan va tra ve thong bao: "Bai viet nay da bi dong bang thao luan boi ban quan tri". Khung soan thao bi vo hieu hoa tren giao dien.
