## Outcome & Scope

- Actor: End User (Voter/Subscriber), Product Manager, System Administrator, External System (Slack, Discord, Jira, GitHub Webhook Consumers).
- Trigger: Co su kien bien dong trong he thong nhu tao bai moi, thay doi trang thai lo trinh, gop bai, co binh luan moi, hoac xuat ban phien ban moi; hoac quan tri vien can tra cuu nhat ky van hanh.
- Job to be done: Dong bo thong tin da chieu giua san pham va nguoi dung (qua thong bao trong ung dung va email), ket noi tien trinh phat trien toi cac he thong ben ngoai (qua Webhooks), dong thoi dam bao tinh minh bach va trach nhiem giai trinh thong qua so kiem toan.
- Successful outcome: Nguoi dung nhan duoc thong bao tuc thi khi tinh nang minh quan tam co tien do moi; he thong ngoai nhan duoc goi tin Webhook dung dinh dang kem chu ky hop le; moi thao tac quan tri duoc luu vet chi tiet voi dia chi IP va thoi gian thuc thi.
- In scope:
  - Co che tu dong dang ky nhan tin (Auto-Subscription): Nguoi tao bai, nguoi bo phieu, va nguoi binh luan tu dong duoc them vao `post_subscriptions`.
  - Nguoi dung chu dong bat tat thong bao in-app hoac email tren tung bai viet cu the.
  - Trung tam thong bao trong ung dung (In-app Notification Center): Hien thi chuong thong bao, so luong tin chua doc, danh sach thong bao theo thoi gian, va danh dau da doc.
  - Phat thong bao da kenh theo loai: STATUS_CHANGED, NEW_COMMENT, OFFICIAL_RESPONSE, FEATURE_RELEASED, MENTIONED.
  - Dang ky va quan ly Webhook (WebhookSubscription): Nhap URL nhan su kien, tao khoa bi mat (`secret_key`), chon danh sach su kien can lang nghe (`POST_CREATED`, `POST_STATUS_CHANGED`, `POST_MERGED`, `COMMENT_ADDED`, `RELEASE_PUBLISHED`).
  - Co che phat Webhook an toan: Dong goi JSON payload, tao chu ky `X-RoadmapHub-Signature` bang HMAC SHA-256, va co che thu lai (Exponential Backoff Retry) khi dau cuoi ben ngoai bi loi.
  - So kiem toan quan tri (AuditLog): Ghi nhat ky bat bien cac thao tac nhay cam (doi trang thai, gop bai, doi quyen thanh vien, xoa phan hoi) kem du lieu JSON diff truoc va sau khi thay doi.
- Out of scope:
  - Tich hop cong thong bao qua SMS hoac tin nhan OTT Zalo/Telegram (se danh cho ban mo rong doanh nghiep).
  - Webhook hai chieu nhan nguoc hanh dong tu Slack ve he thong (Interactive Slack Buttons).

## Context & Constraints

- Preconditions: Workspace dang hoat dong; de nhan thong bao in-app, nguoi dung phai co tai khoan User hop le gan voi phien lam viec.
- Source of truth: Bang `post_subscriptions`, `notifications`, `webhook_subscriptions`, va `audit_logs`.
- Permissions & ownership:
  - Nguoi dung so huu thong bao chi co the xem va danh dau da doc cac ban ghi `notifications` cua chinh minh.
  - Chi Owner va Admin moi co quyen cau hinh Webhooks va xem toan bo nhat ky trong AuditLog.
- Consequential actions:
  - Cac tac vu phat thong bao so luong lon va goi Webhook ra ben ngoai phai thuc hien bat dong bo de tranh gay tre cho nguoi dung cuoi khi tuong tac voi ung dung.
  - Ban ghi trong `audit_logs` la bat bien (Immutable), khong ho tro thao tac cap nhat hoac xoa thu cong tu phia giao dien quan tri.

## State Model

| State | Meaning | Permitted Operator Actions | Terminal / Exit Condition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Notification Unread | Thong bao moi duoc tao, nguoi dung chua mo xem | Nhan vao de doc hoac danh dau da doc | Chuyen sang Read khi nhan vao |
| Notification Read | Thong bao da duoc nguoi dung xem qua | Co the xoa bo khoi hop thu | Luu tru hoac xoa boi nguoi dung |
| Webhook Delivery Pending | Goi tin su kien vua duoc dua vao hang doi gui | Hang doi dang chuan bi thuc thi goi tin | Chuyen sang Delivered hoac Retrying |
| Webhook Delivered | Dau cuoi ben ngoai phan hoi ma HTTP 2xx thanh cong | Ghi nhan nhat ky thanh cong | Ket thuc chu ky phat tin |
| Webhook Retrying | Dau cuoi tra ve loi 5xx hoac timeout, he thong dang cho thu lai | Thu lai theo lich trinh (sau 1m, 5m, 15m) | Chuyen sang Delivered hoac Failed |
| Webhook Failed | Sau 5 lan thu lai lien tiep khong thanh cong | Danh dau that bai, bao dong cho Admin | Ket thuc va luu log loi |

## Primary Path (Happy Path)

### Luong Tu Dong Theo Doi Va Phat Thong Bao Tien Do

- Step Initiation:
  - User Action: Khach hang bo phieu ung ho cho tinh nang "Ho tro Dark Mode tren Mobile".
  - System Response:
    - He thong kiem tra bang `post_subscriptions`. Vi khach hang chua dang ky theo doi bai nay, he thong tu dong tao ban ghi moi voi `postId`, `voterProfileId`, `notify_in_app = true`, va `notify_email = true`.
    - Nut "Theo Doi" tren bai viet tu dong chuyen sang trang thai da kich hoat.

- Step Configuration:
  - User Action: Hai tuan sau, Product Manager chuyen trang thai bai nay sang "Completed" sau khi tinh nang duoc phat hanh.
  - System Response:
    - Backend phat su kien `POST_STATUS_CHANGED`.
    - Hang doi cong viec truy van toan bo nguoi dung co ban ghi trong `post_subscriptions` cua bai nay.
    - Voi moi nguoi dung co lien ket `userId`, hang doi tao ban ghi moi trong `notifications` voi `type = STATUS_CHANGED`, tieu de "Tinh nang ban quan tam da hoan thanh", va duong dan `action_url` den bai viet.
    - Neu nguoi dung co `notify_email = true`, he thong tong hop mau email thong bao va day vao hang doi gui thu.

- Step Confirmation & Completion:
  - User Action: Khach hang mo cong thong tin, nhin thay huy hieu chuong thong bao co cham do hien thi "1". Khach hang bam vao chuong, nhan vao thong bao va duoc chuyen truc tiep den bai viet de xem thong tin phat hanh.
  - System Response: Ban ghi `notifications` duoc cap nhat `is_read = true`, cham do tren huy hieu bien mat.

### Luong Phat Su Kien Webhook Toi Doi Tac Ben Ngoai

- Step Initiation:
  - User Action: Doi ngu phat trien cau hinh mot Webhook endpoint tro den may chu bot Slack cua cong ty (`https://hooks.slack.com/...`), chon su kien `POST_CREATED` va `POST_STATUS_CHANGED`.
  - System Response: He thong luu cau hinh vao `webhook_subscriptions` va tao mot `secretKey` ngau nhien co do dai 32 ky tu.

- Step Configuration:
  - User Action: Co mot khach hang gui mot gop y moi ve tinh nang "Tich hop cong thanh toan Apple Pay".
  - System Response:
    - Backend ghi nhan bai dang thanh cong va day su kien `POST_CREATED` vao hang doi Webhook.
    - Hang doi doc payload chi tiet cua bai dang (id, title, content, author, board).
    - Hang doi su dung thuat toan HMAC SHA-256 voi `secretKey` de tao chu ky ma hoa chuoi payload, gan vao tieu de `X-RoadmapHub-Signature: sha256=...`.

- Step Confirmation & Completion:
  - User Action: He thong thuc hien goi HTTP POST toi URL cua Slack bot kem tieu de xac thuc va payload JSON.
  - System Response: May chu nhan phan hoi ma HTTP 200 OK trong vong 200ms. He thong danh dau goi tin thanh cong trong nhat ky gui Webhook. Tin nhan thong bao gop y moi xuat hien ngay tren kenh chat Slack cua doi ngu san pham.

## Exception & Recovery Paths

- Webhook Endpoint Unreachable / Timeout Path:
  - Trigger: May chu ben ngoai cua doi tac bi sap hoac phan hoi qua cham vuot qua nguong 5000ms.
  - System Behavior: Goi tin bi danh dau la loi timeout. Hang doi tu dong dua vao chu ky thu lai (Retry Schedule) sau 1 phut, 5 phut, 15 phut, va 1 gio. Neu qua 5 lan thu lai van that bai, endpoint se tam thoi bi danh dau canh bao tren giao dien quan tri de Admin kiem tra lai duong dan.

- Unsubscribed User Suppression Path:
  - Trigger: Nguoi dung da chu dong tat thong bao email tren bai viet (`notify_email = false`), nhung he thong van co su kien cap nhat trang thai.
  - System Behavior: Hang doi xu ly van tao thong bao in-app neu co bat co nay, nhung triet de bo qua buoc tao tac vu gui email, dam bao ton trong tuy chon quyen rieng tu cua khach hang.

- Audit Log Persistence Failure Path:
  - Trigger: Xay ra loi ket noi khi ghi nhat ky AuditLog trong mot thao tac quan tri nhay cam (nhu xoa bai dang).
  - System Behavior: Vi AuditLog la rang buoc bat buoc ve mat tuan thu, giao dich goc cua thao tac quan tri cung bi rollback, khong cho phep hanh dong dien ra ma khong co nhat ky kiem toan di kem.
