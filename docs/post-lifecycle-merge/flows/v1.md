## Outcome & Scope

- Actor: Product Manager, Workspace Administrator, Moderator.
- Trigger: Doi ngu quan ly san pham cap nhat tien do phat trien cua mot yeu cau tinh nang, hoac phat hien hai hay nhieu bai dang co noi dung tuong tu nhau can duoc gom lai thanh mot de tap trung luot binh chon va nguoi theo doi.
- Job to be done: Cung cap cong cu quan ly chu trinh song cua tinh nang tu khau tiep nhan den khi hoan tat, dong thoi giai quyet triet de tinh trang phan manh phan hoi bang dong co gop bai nguyen tu bao toan toan ven du lieu.
- Successful outcome: Trang thai tinh nang duoc cap nhat kem nhat ky giai trinh; khi gop bai, bai phu duoc gan trang thai MERGED, toan bo so phieu khong trung lap va nguoi dang ky duoc chuyen giao nguyen ven sang bai chinh, nguoi dung truy cap bai phu duoc tu dong dieu huong sang bai chinh.
- In scope:
  - Quan ly danh muc trang thai tuy bien (Custom Post Statuses) voi cac danh muc chuan: BACKLOG, UNSCHEDULED, PLANNED, IN_PROGRESS, COMPLETED, CLOSED, DECLINED.
  - Cau hinh hien thi trang thai len bang lo trinh (`show_on_roadmap`) va mau sac nhan dien (`color_hex`).
  - Cap nhat trang thai bai dang kem ghi chu ly do (`change_note`) va luu lich su vao `post_status_histories`.
  - Giao dien tim kiem va chon bai viet dich (Target Post) de thuc hien gop bai phu (Source Post).
  - Dong co gop bai nguyen tu (Post Merge Engine):
    - Chuyen nhieu luot bo phieu tu bai B sang bai A (loai tru cac VoterProfile da vote ca hai bai).
    - Tinh toan lai `totalVotesCount` va `totalScoreWeight` cua bai A.
    - Chuyen toan bo ban ghi theo doi `post_subscriptions` tu bai B sang bai A.
    - Cap nhat bai B: gan `merged_into_post_id = A.id` va chuyen trang thai sang loai da gop.
    - Ghi nhat ky vao `post_merge_histories` va `audit_logs`.
    - Phat thong bao den tac gia va nguoi theo doi bai B ve viec gop bai.
- Out of scope:
  - Thao tac hoan tac gop bai (Unmerge) trong phien ban hien tai (do tinh phuc tap cua viec hoan tra trong so phieu da hoa nhap).
  - Gop dong thoi mot danh sach tren 10 bai trong mot lan click (se thuc hien gop don le tung cap bai).

## Context & Constraints

- Preconditions: Chi tai khoan co vai tro Admin hoac Product Manager moi co quyen thay doi trang thai va thuc hien gop bai; bai viet bi gop khong the la chinh no va chua tung bi gop vao mot bai khac truoc do.
- Source of truth: Bang `post_statuses`, `posts`, `post_status_histories`, `post_merge_histories`, `votes`, va `post_subscriptions`.
- Permissions & ownership:
  - Chi Admin va Product Manager co quyen thuc thi gop bai va doi trang thai.
  - Developer co the xem lich su chuyen trang thai va ghi chu gop bai.
- Consequential actions:
  - Moi thay doi trang thai deu tao ban ghi `post_status_histories` va phat su kien `POST_STATUS_CHANGED`.
  - Thao tac gop bai la hanh dong khong the dao nguoc (Irreversible), yeu cau modal xac nhan hai buoc co nhap lai tieu de bai viet de phong tranh sai sot.

## State Model

| State | Meaning | Permitted Operator Actions | Terminal / Exit Condition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Status: Backlog | Yeu cau moi tiep nhan, dang duoc xem xet ban dau | Chuyen sang Planned, In Progress hoac Declined | Chuyen sang trang thai tiep theo |
| Status: Planned | Tinh nang da duoc phe duyet va dua vao ke hoach trien khai | Dua vao lo trinh Kanban, phan bo Sprint | Chuyen sang In Progress |
| Status: In Progress | Tinh nang dang duoc doi ngu ky thuat lap trinh | Keo tha tren Kanban, cap nhat ghi chu tien do | Chuyen sang Completed hoac Closed |
| Status: Completed | Tinh nang da duoc phat trien xong, san sang dua vao ban phat hanh | Gan vao ReleaseVersion, cong bo Changelog | Dong hoan toan |
| Status: Declined | Tinh nang bi tu choi do khong phu hop voi dinh huong san pham | Nhap ly do tu choi de thong bao nguoi dung | Co the mo lai neu dinh huong thay doi |
| Post Flag: Merged | Bai dang da bi hop nhat vao bai khac | Chi xem duoc thong tin, cam thao tac vote/comment | Trang thai ket thuc vinh vien |

## Primary Path (Happy Path)

### Luong Chuyen Doi Trang Thai Tinh Nang (Status Transition)

- Step Initiation:
  - User Action: Product Manager xem chi tiet mot bai dang yeu cau "Bo loc nang cao tren bang du lieu" va nhan nut Chuyen Trang Thai.
  - System Response: He thong mo Dialog cap nhat trang thai, hien thi trang thai hien tai (Planned).

- Step Configuration:
  - User Action: PM chon trang thai moi la "In Progress" (Dang Thuc Hien), nhap ghi chu: "Doi ngu Frontend da bat dau trien khai giao dien bo loc trong Sprint 14".
  - System Response: He thong hien thi danh sach 42 nguoi dung se nhan duoc thong bao tien do nay.

- Step Confirmation & Completion:
  - User Action: PM bam Xac Nhan Cap Nhat.
  - System Response:
    - Backend mo Database Transaction.
    - Cap nhat truong `statusId` tren bang `posts`.
    - Chen ban ghi moi vao `post_status_histories` ghi ro `oldStatusId`, `newStatusId`, `changedById`, va `changeNote`.
    - Tao ban ghi `audit_logs`.
    - Commit Transaction.
    - Day su kien `POST_STATUS_CHANGED` vao hang doi thong bao (In-app notification va email).

### Luong Gop Bai Viet Trung Lap (Post Merge Engine)

- Step Initiation:
  - User Action: PM phat hien bai B ("Can tinh nang xuat file excel") trung voi bai A ("Ho tro xuat du lieu ra XLS va CSV"). PM mo menu tuy chon cua bai B va chon "Gop Vao Bai Khac".
  - System Response: He thong mo Drawer Hop Nhat Bai Dang.

- Step Configuration:
  - User Action: PM nhap ma ID hoac tim kiem tieu de bai A. PM chon bai A lam bai dich.
  - System Response:
    - He thong kiem tra tinh hop le: Bai B khong phai la bai A; bai B chua bi gop; bai A dang hoat dong binh thuong.
    - He thong tinh toan truoc ban xem truoc hop nhat (Merge Preview):
      - Bai B dang co 12 votes, trong do co 3 nguoi dung da vote ca o bai A. So vote se thuc chuyen la 9 votes.
      - Tong diem trong so du kien tang them: 14.00 diem.
      - So nguoi dang ky moi se duoc chuyen sang bai A: 8 nguoi.

- Step Confirmation & Completion:
  - User Action: PM doc ban xem truoc va bam Xac Nhan Gop Bai.
  - System Response:
    - Backend bat dau Atomic Database Transaction.
    - Chuyen cac ban ghi `Vote` hop le tu bai B sang bai A; xoa bo cac vote trung lap cua cung mot VoterProfile.
    - Tinh toan va cap nhat lai `totalVotesCount` va `totalScoreWeight` tren bai A.
    - Chuyen doi toan bo ban ghi `post_subscriptions` tu bai B sang bai A (bo qua nguoi da dang ky bai A).
    - Cap nhat bai B: set `mergedIntoPostId = A.id`, cap nhat co khoa thao luan `is_locked = true`.
    - Chen ban ghi vao `post_merge_histories` voi `sourcePostId = B.id`, `targetPostId = A.id`, va `votesTransferred = 9`.
    - Ghi nhat ky kiem toan vao `audit_logs`.
    - Commit Transaction thanh cong.
    - Gui thong bao den tac gia va nguoi theo doi bai B thong bao bai da duoc hop nhat thanh cong vao bai A.

## Exception & Recovery Paths

- Self or Circular Merge Path:
  - Trigger: Quan tri vien vo tinh chon bai dich trung voi bai nguon hoac chon mot bai ma truoc do da duoc gop vao bai nguon.
  - System Behavior: Backend chan thao tac ngay lap tuc va bao loi: "Khong the gop bai viet vao chinh no hoac tao chu trinh gop bai vong tron". Drawer giu nguyen de nguoi dung chon lai bai hop le.

- Target Post Already Merged Path:
  - Trigger: Nguoi dung chon bai A lam bai dich, nhung bai A truoc do da bi gop vao bai C.
  - System Behavior: He thong canh bao: "Bai viet dich da duoc hop nhat vao bai khac. He thong khong ho tro gop da tang", dong thoi goi y tu dong huong den bai C la goc cuoi cung cua cay hop nhat.

- Database Deadlock / Concurrency Timeout:
  - Trigger: Xay ra xung dot khoa ban ghi khi co nhieu nguoi dung dang vote vao ca hai bai A va B dung thoi diem quan tri vien thuc hien gop bai.
  - System Behavior: Transaction bi huy va rollback toan bo. Giao dien hien thong bao: "He thong dang co bien dong du lieu tren bai viet nay, vui long thu lai sau vai giay". Khong co du lieu vote nao bi that thoat hay tinh sai.
