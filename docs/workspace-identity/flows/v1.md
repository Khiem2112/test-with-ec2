## Outcome & Scope

- Actor: Workspace Owner, Workspace Administrator, Internal Team Member (Product Manager, Developer, Moderator), End Voter (Customer, Guest).
- Trigger: Nguoi dung khoi tao to chuc moi, quan tri vien thiet lap nhan dien thuong hieu, moi thanh vien noi bo vao doi ngu, hoac khach hang truy cap cong gop y cong khai de thiet lap dinh danh.
- Job to be done: Cung cap he thong quan tri da khong gian lam viec hoan chinh voi co che phan quyen RBAC nghiem ngat va ha tang dinh danh nguoi dung linh hoat, dam bao du lieu khong bi ro ri giua cac to chuc.
- Successful outcome: Khong gian lam viec duoc khoi tao voi ten mien/slug rieng biet; cac thanh vien noi bo duoc gan vai tro chinh xac; khach hang ben ngoai duoc nhan dien dong nhat qua VoterProfile de thuc hien bo phieu va dong gop y kien.
- In scope:
  - Khoi tao khong gian lam viec moi, cap nhat ten mien tuy chinh (custom domain), cau hinh logo, mau sac thuong hieu (branding config JSON) va bat tat cong gop y cong khai.
  - Quan ly thanh vien noi bo, gui loi moi tham gia, phan quyen vai tro theo RBAC (Owner, Admin, Product Manager, Developer, Moderator) va thu hoi quyen truy cap.
  - Dinh danh va quan ly VoterProfile qua ba co che: Registered User (tai khoan he thong), SSO Identified (JWT tu san pham ngoai), va Anonymous Fingerprint (ma bam thiet bi ket hop dia chi IP).
  - Kiem soat phien lam viec (UserSession) va kiem tra tinh hop le cua token.
- Out of scope:
  - Tich hop cong thanh toan hoa don dang ky goi cuoc SaaS cho Workspace (thuoc phan he Subscription Billing sau nay).
  - Xac thuc da yeu to nang cao (FIDO2/Hardware Key).

## Context & Constraints

- Preconditions: Nguoi dung tao Workspace phai la tai khoan he thong da xac thuc email; nguoi duoc moi vao Workspace phai co email hop le.
- Source of truth: Bang `workspaces`, `workspace_members`, `users`, `voter_profiles`, va `user_sessions`.
- Permissions & ownership:
  - Chi Owner moi co quyen xoa Workspace hoac chuyen quyen so huu to chuc.
  - Owner va Admin co toan quyen thay doi cau hinh nhan dien thuong hieu, ten mien tuy chinh va quan ly thanh vien noi bo.
  - Cac vai tro Product Manager, Developer, Moderator chi co quyen truy cap pham vi nghiep vu tuong ung, khong duoc thay doi cau hinh to chuc.
- Consequential actions:
  - Xoa thanh vien hoac thu hoi quyen truy cap phai ngay lap tuc vo hieu hoa cac phien dang nhap va ghi nhat ky AuditLog.
  - Thay doi slug hoac custom domain phai duoc xac thuc tranh xung dot khoa duy nhat tren he thong.

## Primary Path (Happy Path)

### Luong Khoi Tao Khong Gian Lam Viec Va Thiet Lap Nhan Dien Thuong Hieu

- Step Initiation:
  - User Action: Quan tri vien dang nhap vao he thong va nhan nut Tao Khong Gian Lam Viec.
  - System Response: He thong hien thi bieu mau khoi tao yeu cau nhap Ten Workspace va Slug dai dien.
  - Available Choices: Nhap thong tin hoac huy bo thao tac quay ve trang chu.

- Step Configuration:
  - User Action: Quan tri vien nhap ten san pham (vi du: AutoFlow Hub), slug tu dong duoc tao theo dang kebab-case (`autoflow-hub`). Quan tri vien chon mau chu dao va tai len logo to chuc.
  - System Response: He thong kiem tra tinh duy nhat cua slug theo thoi gian thuc tren bang `workspaces`, hien thi ban xem truoc giao dien cong khai (Live Branding Preview).

- Step Confirmation & Completion:
  - User Action: Quan tri vien xac nhan Tao Khong Gian Lam Viec.
  - System Response: He thong tao ban ghi trong bang `workspaces`, tu dong them quan tri vien hien tai vao bang `workspace_members` voi vai tro `OWNER`, khoi tao cac trang thai bai dang mac dinh, va dieu huong vao Bang Dieu Khien Quan Tri (`/admin/workspaces/autoflow-hub/dashboard`).

### Luong Moi Thanh Vien Noi Bo Va Phan Quyen RBAC

- Step Initiation:
  - User Action: Owner hoac Admin truy cap the Quan Ly Thanh Vien va nhan nut Moi Thanh Vien.
  - System Response: He thong mo Drawer bieu mau moi thanh vien.

- Step Configuration:
  - User Action: Quan tri vien nhap dia chi email cua nhan su, lua chon vai tro tu danh sach (Product Manager, Developer, Moderator).
  - System Response: He thong kiem tra dinh dang email va xac minh xem tai khoan da thuoc ve Workspace nay hay chua.

- Step Confirmation & Completion:
  - User Action: Quan tri vien nhan nut Gui Loi Moi.
  - System Response: He thong luu thong tin vao `workspace_members`, tao ban ghi kiem toan trong `audit_logs`, va gui thong bao den email cua nhan su duoc moi.

### Luong Dinh Danh Khach Hang (Voter Profile Resolution)

- Step Initiation:
  - User Action: Khach hang truy cap cong gop y cong khai cua Workspace va thuc hien hanh dong bo phieu hoac gui gop y.
  - System Response: He thong kiem tra thong tin dinh danh trong phien truy cap.

- Step Configuration:
  - User Action: Neu khach hang chua dang nhap, he thong kiem tra tieu de xac thuc SSO JWT (neu duoc nhung tu ung dung ben ngoai) hoac tao ma bam thiet bi tu Device Fingerprint va dia chi IP salt.
  - System Response: He thong truy van bang `voter_profiles` theo `(workspace_id, external_user_id)` hoac `(workspace_id, fingerprint_hash)`.

- Step Confirmation & Completion:
  - User Action: Khach hang tiep tuc hanh dong voi ho so dinh danh da duoc xac dinh.
  - System Response: Neu chua ton tai, he thong tu dong tao ban ghi `VoterProfile` moi voi cap bac khach hang mac dinh (`standard`) va trong so `vote_weight = 1.00`, gan phien lam viec de tiep tuc luu tru gop y.

## Exception & Recovery Paths

- Validation Error Path:
  - Trigger: Slug khong gian lam viec bi trung lap voi to chuc da ton tai trong co so du lieu.
  - System Behavior: Hien thi thong bao loi mau do ngay duoi o nhap slug: "Duong dan dinh danh da duoc su dung, vui long chon ten khac", dong thoi giu nguyen cac thong tin con lai tren bieu mau de nguoi dung chinh sua.

- Unauthorized Access Path:
  - Trigger: Nguoi dung co vai tro Developer co gang truy cap trang thay doi ten mien tuy chinh hoac xoa khong gian lam viec.
  - System Behavior: He thong chan yeu cau tai tang Middleware, tra ve ma loi HTTP 403 Forbidden kem thong bao: "Ban khong co quyen thuc hien thao tac quan tri nay", dieu huong nguoi dung ve trang tong quan.

- Failure & Timeout Path:
  - Trigger: Mat ket noi co so du lieu khi dang thuc hien luu cau hinh branding hoac cap nhat vai tro thanh vien.
  - System Behavior: He thong thuc hien rollback giao dich, hien thi banner canh bao loi ket noi he thong, cung cap nut Thu Lai (Retry) ma khong lam mat du lieu nguoi dung da nhap.
