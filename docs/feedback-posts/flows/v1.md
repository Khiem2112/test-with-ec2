## Outcome & Scope

- Actor: End User (Khach hang, Nguoi dung cuoi), Product Manager, Moderator (Kiem duyet vien).
- Trigger: Nguoi dung co nhu cau de xuat tinh nang moi hoac bao cao su co loi ky thuat tren cong thong tin RoadmapHub.
- Job to be done: Giup nguoi dung de dang truyen dat y tuong hoac van de gap phai, tu dong phat hien cac yeu cau tuong tu da co san de uu tien gom luot binh chon, va to chuc du lieu theo danh muc, nhan he thong mot cach ro rang.
- Successful outcome: Nguoi dung tim thay bai tuong tu va thuc hien bo phieu ngay lap tuc, hoac gui thanh cong mot bai dang moi chua day du mo ta, danh muc va anh dinh kem; bai dang duoc gan trang thai tiep nhan khoi diem va hien thi dung tren bang phan hoi tuong ung.
- In scope:
  - Xem danh sach cac bang chuyen de (Boards: Core Features, Mobile App, Bug Reports, Integrations).
  - Loc bai dang theo danh muc (Categories), nhan gan kem (Tags) va trang thai xu ly.
  - Bieu mau tao bai dang thong minh voi chuc nang goi y bai trung lap tuc thi dua tren thuat toan chuoi ky tu (Trigram / Full-text search).
  - Nhanh re huong: Nguoi dung chuyen sang bo phieu cho bai da co san hoac tiep tuc gui bai viet moi.
  - Tai len va quan ly tep dinh kem (anh chup man hinh, tai lieu dac ta) gan voi bai dang.
  - Tu dong thiet lap theo doi tien do cho tac gia bai viet qua bang `post_subscriptions`.
  - Che do kiem duyet bai dang (is_moderated) doi voi cac bang yeu cau phe duyet truoc khi cong khai.
- Out of scope:
  - Chinh sua sau khi bai viet da co binh luan tu thanh vien khac (se gioi han chi sua trong 15 phut dau).
  - Tich hop cong cu ve truc tiep tren anh chup man hinh (annotation tools).

## Context & Constraints

- Preconditions: Khach hang da xac dinh duoc VoterProfile (tai khoan dang ky, SSO, hoac fingerprint an danh); Workspace va Board o trang thai hoat dong (`is_active = true`).
- Source of truth: Bang `boards`, `categories`, `tags`, `post_tags`, `posts`, `post_attachments`, va `post_subscriptions`.
- Permissions & ownership:
  - Moi nguoi dung deu co the xem va tim kiem bai tren cac bang cong khai (`is_public = true`).
  - Nguoi dung tao bai duoc so huu bai dang cua minh va co quyen chinh sua tieu de, noi dung trong khoang thoi gian quy dinh neu bai chua bi khoa (`is_locked = false`).
  - Moderator, Product Manager, va Admin co toan quyen ghim bai (`is_pinned`), khoa thao luan (`is_locked`), hoac xoa mem bai vi pham.
- Consequential actions:
  - Khi bai dang duoc tao thanh cong, he thong phai tu dong tao ban ghi trong `post_subscriptions` cho tac gia de bat dau nhan thong bao cap nhat.
  - Neu Board bat che do kiem duyet, bai dang duoc gan trang thai tam giu va khong xuat hien tren feed cong khai cho den khi duoc duyet.

## Primary Path (Happy Path)

### Luong Nguoi Dung Gui Gop Y Va Kiem Tra Trung Lap Tu Dong

- Step Initiation:
  - User Action: Khach hang truy cap vao Bang Tinh Nang Cot Loi (Core Features) va nhan nut Gui Y Kien Gop Y.
  - System Response: He thong mo Dialog tao bai viet moi, con tro chuot tu dong tap trung vao o nhap tieu de.
  - Available Choices: Nhap tieu de hoac dong modal de tiep tuc luot xem.

- Step Configuration:
  - User Action: Nguoi dung go vao o tieu de: "Cho phep dang nhap bang tai khoan Google".
  - System Response: Sau khi nguoi dung ngung go 300ms (Debounce), he thong gui yeu cau goi API tim kiem tuong dong (`GET /api/posts/search-similar?boardId=...&q=...`). Phia server dung PostgreSQL Trigram danh gia chi so giong nhau va tra ve ket qua.
  - Available Choices:
    - Nhanh 1: Nguoi dung nhin thay bai "Tich hop Google SSO Login" da co san tren he thong voi 45 votes. Nguoi dung nhan nut Bo Phieu Cho Bai Nay. He thong thuc hien ghi nhan upvote cho bai da ton tai, tu dong dong dialog va hien thong bao thanh cong: "Ban da dong gop luot binh chon cho y tuong da co!".
    - Nhanh 2: Nguoi dung nhan thay y tuong cua minh la moi hoac khong trung lap, tiep tuc bo qua danh sach goi y va nhap chi tiet mo ta vao hop Markdown.

- Step Confirmation & Completion:
  - User Action: Nguoi dung chon Danh Muc "Xac Thuc & Bao Mat", chon tep anh minh hoa tai len tu may tinh, va nhan nut Gui Yeu Cau.
  - System Response:
    - He thong luu thong tin bai viet vao bang `posts`, luu thong tin file vao bang `post_attachments`.
    - Gan trang thai tiep nhan ban dau (Status: Backlog / Under Review).
    - Tu dong them mot ban ghi vao `post_subscriptions` voi `notify_in_app = true` va `notify_email = true` cho nguoi tao.
    - Day su kien `POST_CREATED` vao hang doi de phat Webhook den kenh noi bo (Slack/Discord cua doi ngu san pham).
    - Hien thi thong bao thanh cong va dieu huong nguoi dung toi trang chi tiet cua bai dang vua tao.

## Exception & Recovery Paths

- Exact Duplicate Title Warning Path:
  - Trigger: Nguoi dung nhap tieu de giong 100% mot bai dang da ton tai tren cung mot Board.
  - System Behavior: Hien thi hop thoai canh bao do: "Tieu de nay da ton tai chinh xac tren bang. Vui long kiem tra bai viet hien co de tranh trung lap noi dung", kem nut xem truc tiep bai dang da ton tai.

- Attachment Size Exceeded Path:
  - Trigger: Nguoi dung tai len tep dinh kem co dung luong vuot qua 10MB hoac dinh dang khong phai anh hop le (chi cho phep PNG, JPG, WEBP, PDF).
  - System Behavior: He thong tu choi tep tai phia trinh duyet va hien thong bao loi: "Tep tai len vuot qua dung luong toi da 10MB hoac khong dung dinh dang ho tro". O tai tep duoc reset ma khong lam mat tieu de va noi dung mo ta da nhap.

- Moderated Board Pending Approval Path:
  - Trigger: Nguoi dung gui bai tren bang co thiet lap kiem duyet truoc (`is_moderated = true`).
  - System Behavior: Bai dang duoc ghi nhan voi co `is_active = false`. He thong hien thong bao huong dan: "Gop y cua ban da duoc gui thanh cong va dang duoc doi ngu kiem duyet truoc khi xuat hien tren bang cong khai". Nguoi dung nhan duoc thong bao khi bai duoc duyet.
