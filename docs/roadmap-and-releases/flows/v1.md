- Status: Pending

## Outcome & Scope

- Actor: Public Customer / Voter (Nguoi dung theo doi tien do), Product Manager, Tech Lead.
- Trigger: Nguoi dung muon xem tong quan dinh huong va tien do phat trien san pham, hoac Product Manager can to chuc ke hoach phat hanh, dong goi tinh nang da xong vao ban phat hanh va xuat ban Changelog.
- Job to be done: Mang lai su minh bach toi da giua doi ngu san pham va cong dong khach hang, giup khach hang biet ro khi nao tinh nang minh quan tam se ra mat, dong thoi giup doi ngu san pham truyen thong hieu qua gia tri cua moi ban cap nhat.
- Successful outcome: Khach hang de dang theo doi tien do qua giao dien Kanban lo trinh; Product Manager quan ly cac cot moc phat hanh ro rang va xuat ban thanh cong nhat ky thay doi co dinh kem danh sach tinh nang da trien khai thuc te.
- In scope:
  - Bieu dien bang lo trinh Kanban truc quan theo cac cot trang thai (vi du: Under Review, Planned, In Progress, Completed).
  - Tich hop keo tha the tinh nang (Drag-and-Drop) danh cho Product Manager de cap nhat tien do nhanh chong.
  - Cau hinh goc nhin lo trinh linh hoat thong qua bang `roadmap_views` (chon cot hien thi, sap xep thu tu).
  - Bo loc the tren lo trinh theo Board chuyen de, Category ky thuat, va Tags.
  - Quan ly phien ban phat hanh (ReleaseVersion): Tao phien ban moi (vi du: v1.2.0), thiet lap ngay muc tieu (target_date), va ngay phat hanh thuc te (released_at).
  - Gan the tinh nang da hoan thanh vao phien ban phat hanh thong qua bang lien ket `release_items`.
  - Soan thao va xuat ban bai viet nhat ky thay doi (Changelog): Ho tro trinh soan thao Markdown, gan anh bia, tom tat ngan gon, va ban xem truoc thoi gian thuc.
  - Trang Nhat ky thay doi cong khai (`/[workspaceSlug]/changelog`) kem nut dang ky nhan email ban tin phat hanh.
- Out of scope:
  - Tich hop cong cu tu dong phan tich Git Commit / Pull Request de sinh Changelog tu dong (se xem xet tich hop voi GitHub o phien ban nang cao).
  - Giao dien bieu do Gantt theo thoi gian chi tiet den tung ngay (chi ho tro dang cot Kanban va moc Quy/Sprint).

## Context & Constraints

- Preconditions: Khong gian lam viec da cau hinh it nhat 2 trang thai co `show_on_roadmap = true`.
- Source of truth: Bang `roadmap_views`, `release_versions`, `release_items`, `changelogs`, `posts`, va `post_statuses`.
- Permissions & ownership:
  - Moi nguoi dung deu co the xem bang lo trinh cong khai va trang Changelog neu khong gian lam viec bat che do cong khai (`is_public_portal = true`).
  - Chi Admin va Product Manager co quyen keo tha thay doi trang thai tren Kanban, tao phien ban phat hanh va xuat ban Changelog.
- Consequential actions:
  - Keo mot the tren Kanban sang trang thai moi se kich hoat quy trinh chuyen trang thai giong nhu trong Flow: Post Lifecycle.
  - Xuat ban Changelog voi `is_published = true` se gui thong bao email va in-app toi toan bo nguoi dung da theo doi cac bai dang thuoc phien ban do.

## State Model

| State | Meaning | Permitted Operator Actions | Terminal / Exit Condition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Release Version Planned | Phien ban phat hanh dang duoc len ke hoach, dang gom cac tinh nang | Them/bot tinh nang vao ReleaseItem, doi ngay muc tieu | Chuyen sang Released khi phat hanh |

## Primary Path (Happy Path)

### Luong Dieu Phoi Tien Do Tren Bang Kanban Lo Trinh

- Step Initiation:
  - User Action: Product Manager truy cap Bang Dieu Khien Lo Trinh va nhin thay the tinh nang "Xuat bao cao PDF" dang nam o cot "Planned".
  - System Response: Giao dien hien thi cac cot Kanban tuong ung: Backlog, Planned, In Progress, Completed.

- Step Configuration:
  - User Action: PM su dung chuot keo the tu cot "Planned" tha vao cot "In Progress".
  - System Response: The lap tuc di chuyen sang cot moi tren giao dien (Drag-and-Drop animation).

- Step Confirmation & Completion:
  - User Action: He thong tu dong mo popover hoi nhanh ghi chu tien do (tuy chon, co the bo qua).
  - System Response:
    - Backend cap nhat `statusId` cua bai viet tuong ung voi trang thai cot "In Progress".
    - Ghi nhan lich su vao `post_status_histories`.
    - Phat su kien dong bo toi toan bo nhung nguoi dang xem lo trinh qua Server-Sent Events / WebSocket (neu co).
    - Hang doi gui thong bao tien do den cac subscriber cua bai viet.

### Luong Dong Goi Phien Ban Va Xuat Ban Changelog

- Step Initiation:
  - User Action: Sau khi hoan tat cac tinh nang trong chu ky phat trien, PM truy cap Quan Ly Phien Ban va nhan "Tao Phien Ban Phat Hanh".
  - System Response: He thong mo Dialog tao ban phat hanh.

- Step Configuration:
  - User Action: PM nhap ten phien ban "Release v2.0.0", chon ngay phat hanh la hom nay, va nhan nut "Chon Tinh Nang Da Hoan Thanh". He thong liet ke cac bai dang co trang thai Completed. PM danh dau chon 5 tinh nang chu chot va nhan nut Luu.
  - System Response: He thong tao ban ghi trong `release_versions` va 5 ban ghi tuong ung trong `release_items`.

- Step Confirmation & Completion:
  - User Action: PM nhan nut "Soan Nhat Ky Thay Doi (Changelog)". Giao dien soan thao tu dong dien san danh sach 5 tinh nang duoi dang gach dau dong Markdown. PM viet them phan loi ngo va huong dan su dung, kiem tra ban xem truoc va bam "Xuat Ban Changelog".
  - System Response:
    - He thong luu ban ghi vao bang `changelogs` voi `is_published = true` va `published_at = now()`.
    - Cap nhat `release_versions.is_published = true`.
    - Day su kien `RELEASE_PUBLISHED` vao hang doi de phat Webhook toi Slack/Discord cua to chuc.
    - Gui thong bao email toi toan bo nguoi dung da quan tam den 5 tinh nang duoc phat hanh trong dot nay.
    - Bai viet xuat hien ngay tren trang Changelog cong khai (`/[workspaceSlug]/changelog`).

## Exception & Recovery Paths

- Invalid Card Drop Path:
  - Trigger: PM keo the sang mot cot ma trang thai do bi khoa hoac vi pham quy tac chuyen trang thai (vi du: keo tu Declined ve Completed ma chua qua xem xet).
  - System Behavior: The tu dong bat nguoc tro ve cot ban dau (Revert Drop Animation). He thong hien thong bao canh bao: "Khong the chuyen truc tiep giua hai trang thai nay".

- Empty Changelog Publish Path:
  - Trigger: PM nhan Xuat Ban Changelog khi chua nhap tieu de hoac noi dung than bai duoi 10 ky tu.
  - System Behavior: Nut Xuat Ban bi vo hieu hoa, truong nhap lieu bi danh dau vien do kem thong bao: "Vui long nhap day du tieu de va noi dung tom tat cho ban tin phat hanh".

- Release Version Name Conflict:
  - Trigger: PM tao mot phien ban trung ten voi phien ban da ton tai trong cung Workspace (vi du da co "v1.0.0").
  - System Behavior: Co so du lieu chan theo rang buoc `@@unique([workspaceId, versionName])`. Hien thi thong bao: "Phien ban nay da ton tai trong khong gian lam viec, vui long danh so phien ban khac".
